Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cotton bedding, sizes, delivery, and COD.

How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work? ↓

When you order with Cash on Delivery, you don't need to pay anything online. Our courier partner (TCS, Trax, Leopards, or PostEx) will deliver your parcel directly to your address, and you pay cash to the rider upon receiving your package.

What is the standard delivery time across Pakistan? ↓

For major cities including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi, delivery usually takes 2 to 4 working days. Other cities take 3 to 6 working days. You will receive SMS updates with your consignment tracking number.

Are your bed sheets 100% pure cotton? ↓

Yes! All our bed sheets, duvet covers, and pillowcases are woven with 100% combed long-staple cotton with zero polyester blends. They are naturally breathable, hypoallergenic, and improve in softness with each wash.

What are your King and Queen bed sheet dimensions? ↓

Our King Size flat sheet measures 96 x 100 inches (244 x 254 cm), Queen Size measures 90 x 100 inches, and Single Size measures 60 x 90 inches. Pillowcases are standard 19 x 29 inches. Check our Size Guide for full details.

What is your return & exchange guarantee? ↓

We offer a hassle-free 7-day easy exchange. If you want a different color or size, message our WhatsApp support (+92 300 1234567) and our team will arrange an exchange parcel for you.
